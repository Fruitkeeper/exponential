## How I Work

- **Gets me pumped:** Seeing customers get real value from something we built. Nothing beats a pilot user telling you they can't go back to the old way.
- **Disappoints me:** Slow feedback loops. In research you can wait months to learn if something worked, and that's a big reason I moved toward products, where a customer tells you within days. I'm also frustrated when problems get hidden instead of flagged early.
- **Strengths:** Going from 0 to 1 quickly; owning the full product lifecycle, from customer discovery to spec to shipping; working across the stack from LLM agents to embedded robotics.
- **Improvement points:** I get very intense when something is blocking progress. At Skoll, I chased our CTO for status twice a day on a critical CRM sync, and I was adding pressure instead of help. Now I do one check-in at the start of the day and focus on one question: what exactly is the bottleneck? Then I help unblock it or take other work off the person's plate. I keep the urgency, but I aim it at the problem, not the person.
- **Known behaviours / comms style:** Direct and to the point. Async-first, but always happy to jump on a quick call when that's faster.
